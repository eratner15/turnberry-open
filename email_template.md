## Customization Guide

### For Different Verticals

**Fleet Management:**
- Replace "Body Cameras" with "Fleet Vehicles" or "Fleet Management Software"
- Update signal types: "vehicle replacement," "maintenance software," "GPS tracking"
- Adjust contacts to Fleet Managers, Public Works Directors

**Water Infrastructure:**
- Focus on "Water Treatment," "Pump Stations," "SCADA Systems"
- Signal types: "infrastructure upgrades," "compliance requirements," "system failures"
- Contacts: Water/Wastewater Directors, Public Works Engineers

### Personalization Tokens

Use these merge tags in your email software:
- `[First Name]` - Prospect's first name
- `[Company]` - Their company name
- `[Vertical]` - Their focus area (e.g., "Body Cameras")
- `[Territory]` - Their sales territory
- `[DOWNLOAD_FULL_REPORT_LINK]` - Link to full PDF report
- `[UNSUBSCRIBE_LINK]` - Unsubscribe URL
- `[PREFERENCES_LINK]` - Email preferences URL

### Frequency Options

**Weekly Digest** (Recommended):
- Send every Friday morning
- Aggregate 5-10 signals
- Include timeline for upcoming decisions

**Bi-Weekly Digest**:
- Send 1st and 15th of month
- Deeper analysis of fewer signals
- Include trend analysis

**Real-Time Alerts** (Premium):
- Send within 24 hours of high-confidence signal
- Single-signal focus
- Maximum urgency for time-sensitive opportunities
